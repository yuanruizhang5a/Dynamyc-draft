\section{Local unfolding and cyclic continuation proofs}\label{sec:local-proofs}

The continuation suite opens the current state with \code{unfold_at},
decomposes the regular-program layer, and backlinks when a continuation
goal repeats. It imports proposition-only declarations from \code{Cases/}.
The proof functor transports the generative record wrappers while preserving
description formulas and ordered labelled edges, and reuses the control
type, enumeration, initial state and instructions.
\sources{\rocq{Benchmarks/Support.v}{import_machine},
  \rocq{Benchmarks/Support.v}{imported_description},
  \rocq{Benchmarks/Support.v}{imported_edges}}

\subsection{Named proof contexts}
The finite fragments are
\[
 f\bnf\mathsf{Call}(q)\mid\mathsf{Atom}(\alpha)
       \mid\mathsf{Then}(f,g)\mid\mathsf{Branch}(f,g).
\]
They are proof contexts, not new LPL program constructors. Substitution
$\mathsf{plug}(h,f)$ replaces calls by $h(q)$, keeps atoms, and maps Then
and Branch to ordinary LPL sequence and choice.
\sources{\rocq{Continuations.v}{fragment}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{plug}}

Fix targets $F$. Let $S_q=O_q$ if $q\in F$, and $S_q=\bot?$ otherwise.
Interpret calls at an arbitrary natural depth using ordinary LPL programs:
\begin{align*}
 A_0(q)&=S_q,\\
 A_{n+1}(q)&=\begin{cases}
 S_q\cup\mathsf{unfold\_at}(m,A_n,q),&q\in F,\\
 \mathsf{unfold\_at}(m,A_n,q),&q\notin F.
 \end{cases}
\end{align*}
Depth is a semantic parameter for sound progress, not a chosen benchmark
cutoff. The final graph theorems quantify over \emph{every} depth.
\sources{\rocq{Continuations.v}{selected_spec}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{stop},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{approx}}

The fragment $L_q$ observes $q$ and chooses an outgoing
$\mathsf{instruction}(e);\mathsf{Call}(\mathsf{target}(e))$.
Its selected-target extension $X_q$ also permits the stop atom. Exact
substitution equations connect the fragments to the existing interface:
\[
 \mathsf{plug}(h,L_q)=\mathsf{unfold\_at}(m,h,q),\qquad
 \mathsf{plug}(A_n,X_q)=A_{n+1}(q).
\]
Thus opening a call exposes one layer without constructing a recursive
program value or eliminating the whole graph. Checked syntax and source
observations hold at every depth.
\sources{\rocq{Continuations.v}{plug_layer},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{plug_expansion},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{approx_checked},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{approx_source_observed}}

\subsection{Contract rules and false successors}
A specification supplies the machine, targets, invariant $I$, checked
syntax and safety of each listed instruction. Its endpoint predicate is
$P(s)\iff\exists r\in F.\ [s,s]\sat_M D_r$.
A goal is $(\mathit{mode},f)$, with mode Safety or Contract. Its world is
$(n,\pi)$, a depth and an ordinary LPL trace. The goal fails exactly when
$\pi$ executes $\mathsf{plug}(A_n,f)$, starts in $I$, and violates
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf{good}_{\rm Safety}(\pi)&\iff\mathsf{Forall}(I,\pi),\\
 \mathsf{good}_{\rm Contract}(\pi)&\iff
      \mathsf{Forall}(I,\pi)\land P(\lasts\pi).
\end{align*}
This is a proof-goal denotation over LPL executions, not another ASM
execution relation.
\sources{\rocq{Continuations.v}{SPEC}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{endpoint},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{good}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{task_bad}}

Sequence creates a Safety premise for its prefix and an original-mode
premise for its suffix. Choice creates original-mode premises for both
branches. A bad sequence has an unsafe prefix or a bad suffix starting
at a safe shared endpoint; a bad choice has a bad selected branch.
Every rule record proves these false-successor facts as well as ordinary
preservation of validity.
\sources{\rocq{Continuations.v}{bad_sequence},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{bad_choice}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{local_rule},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{sequence_rule}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{choice_rule}}

Observation and instruction leaves close only after syntax and approved
local-atom checks. A Contract leaf must establish its endpoint predicate.
Model-dependent instruction safety remains a supplied proof. The guarded
decrement uses the existing local test/assignment arithmetic argument,
not a theorem about a compiled machine.
These are semantic contract rules for LPL programs. They form a separate
instance of the generic framework, not the labelled-sequent rule records
of \code{B.Seq.R} and \code{B.Choice.R}. LPL's syntax and semantics remain
unchanged.
\sources{\rocq{Continuations.v}{leafb}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{leaf_axiom},
  \rocq{Support.v}{decrement_safe}}

\subsection{Actual backlinks and checked progress}
Unfolding has conclusion $(\mathit{mode},\mathsf{Call}(q))$ and premise
$(\mathit{mode},X_q)$. A false call cannot occur at depth zero, since its
stop observation has the proved contract. At depth $n+1$, the substitution
equation transfers its bad trace to the expansion at depth $n$.
The followed rank is therefore $n$, with strict decrease on unfolding.
Sequence, choice and exact backlinks preserve that rank. Every goal
advertises tag zero.
\sources{\rocq{Continuations.v}{Theory}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{unfold_rule},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{backlink_rule}}

The builder remembers the first occurrence of each call and executes an
actual \code{AddBacklink} for an identical repeated call. Its work budget
bounds construction, independently of semantic depth. Exhaustion leaves
open nodes that fail checking.
The routing witness uses potential zero at unfolded call nodes, one at
backlink nodes, and fragment size plus two at regular nodes. Syntax
decreases during decomposition; backlinks decrease potential to the
companion's zero. A strict unfolding edge permits the potential to reset.
\sources{\rocq{Continuations.v}{build_work}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{build},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{fragment_size}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{witness}}

The generic raw-check bridge constructs a semantic certificate from all
successful executable checks and invokes the unchanged framework's
\code{certificate_sound}. The accepted graph, rather than a separate
global invariant induction, establishes all-depth safety and endpoint
observations.
\sources{\rocq{GraphCertification.v}{certify_raw_check},
  \rocq{GraphCertification.v}{raw_check_sound},
  \rocq{Continuations.v}{accepted_safe}, \rocq{Continuations.v}{accepted_post}}
