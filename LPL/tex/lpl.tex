\begin{abstract}
This manual presents the mathematical language, models, finite-trace semantics,
labelled sequent calculus, and cyclic certification interface implemented in
\texttt{Rocq-copy/LPL}. It follows the reference-manual organization of
\texttt{iris/tex}: a small master document, separate mathematical sections,
and reusable notation. Definitions and named results are accompanied by
references to their Rocq declarations. The reference covers many-sorted
first-order observations, regular programs, temporal observations of accumulated
traces, sequential and parallel state updates, and the minimum-counterexample
rank used to justify cyclic proofs. It describes the implemented theory,
including its restrictions and proof obligations; it does not assume additional
rules from earlier manuscript drafts.
\end{abstract}
