\section{Source map and maintenance}\label{sec:source-map}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{0.31\textwidth}L{0.63\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule File & Responsibility\\\midrule
\code{Syntax.v} & Proposition-only descriptions, machines, observations,
finite outgoing choice, one-step unfolding and well-formedness.\\
\code{Properties.v} & Local safety and source observation, LPL safety
combinators, final-valuation predicates.\\
\code{Support.v} & Integer model and local execution/arithmetic helpers.\\
\code{Cases/} & Five families of machine declarations.\\
\code{Continuations.v} & Proof contexts, local contract rules, builder,
backlinks and all-depth safety/endpoint soundness.\\
\code{GraphCertification.v} & Generic executable-check reflection.\\
\code{Benchmarks/} & Checked graphs, execution witnesses and
positive/negative regression cases.\\
\code{run_unfold_benchmarks.py} & Standalone timed runner, source audit,
hashes and independent kernel check.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The active source snapshot includes \code{Benchmarks/}, \code{Cases/}
and the top-level theory files. Shared helpers live in \code{Support.v}. Manual checks
resolve all declaration links and compare source and evidence hashes.
After changing code or measured evidence, review this text, run
\code{python3 check_manual.py --refresh}, then \code{make}.
Hash and link checks detect drift; they do not prove prose equivalence.
